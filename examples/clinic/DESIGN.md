# Neighbourhood health clinic

## Brief
A two-storey neighbourhood health clinic on a corner plot in a mixed residential area: general practice,
a small physiotherapy unit and a community room for evening courses. Patients arrive on foot, by bike or
by wheelchair; every public room must be reachable without steps. The clinic should feel like a house in
the street, not a hospital: compact, calm, daylight in every room.

## Ideas
- One straight corridor on each floor, rooms on both sides, the core at the street end.
- Treatment and physiotherapy on the ground floor, consultation upstairs where it is quieter.
- Try a timber variant: lighter, faster to build, maybe cheaper.
